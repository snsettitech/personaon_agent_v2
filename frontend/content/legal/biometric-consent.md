## Definitions and Applicable Laws

### Canadian law

Under the federal *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* ("**PIPEDA**") and Quebec's *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector* (commonly known as "**Law 25**"), biometric data — including voiceprints and facial geometry — is treated as **sensitive personal information**. Express consent is required for its collection, use, and disclosure. Quebec Law 25 imposes additional obligations, including (a) the requirement that an organization conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment before deploying technology that processes biometric data for verification or identification, and (b) prior notice to the *Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec* at least 60 days before the deployment of any system using biometric characteristics for verification or identification (Article 45 of the *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector*).

Alberta's *Personal Information Protection Act* and British Columbia's *Personal Information Protection Act* similarly treat biometric data as sensitive and require meaningful consent.

### U.S. state biometric privacy laws

The Illinois *Biometric Information Privacy Act*, 740 ILCS 14/1 *et seq*. ("**BIPA**"), the Texas *Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act* ("**CUBI**"), the Washington biometric privacy statute (RCW 19.375), the Washington *My Health My Data Act*, and similar laws in other U.S. states regulate the collection, storage, use, disclosure, and retention of biometric identifiers and biometric information.

### Definitions

"**Biometric identifier**" generally means a retina or iris scan, fingerprint, voiceprint, or scan of hand or face geometry. Biometric identifiers do not include items such as photographs themselves, or physical descriptions like height, weight, hair colour, or eye colour.

"**Biometric information**" generally means any information, regardless of how it is captured, converted, stored, or shared, based on an individual's biometric identifier and used to identify the individual.

The terms above may carry different meanings in different jurisdictions. Where a stricter definition applies in your province or state, the stricter definition controls.

## What Biometric Identifiers and Biometric Information We Collect

In connection with your creation and operation of a Persona, PersonaOn (directly and through subprocessors identified below) may derive the following from Creator Content you provide:

- **Voiceprints and voice models.** From audio recordings of your voice, we (and our third-party voice synthesis provider) may extract voice features and train a synthetic voice model that captures the acoustic characteristics of your voice. The features and model may, in combination, be used to identify you as the speaker and therefore may constitute a "voiceprint" or biometric information under applicable law.

- **Facial geometry and avatar models.** From photographs and videos of you, we (and our third-party avatar rendering provider) may extract a scan of facial geometry and other facial measurements used to render an avatar that resembles you. These derivations may constitute biometric identifiers or biometric information under applicable law.

- **Other physiological measures.** Where we render a Persona that includes additional embodied features (for example, gait, posture, or expression mapping), we may derive additional measures intended to capture your likeness in a way that may qualify as biometric information.

We do not collect retina or iris scans or fingerprints, except as may incidentally appear in Creator Content (in which case we do not extract or use them as biometric identifiers).

## Why We Collect This Information (Purpose)

We collect and use the biometric identifiers and biometric information described above for the following purposes only:

1. To generate, render, and operate your Persona on the Services in a manner that resembles you to the extent you have configured;
2. To enable the Persona to interact with End Users using your synthesized voice and avatar;
3. To improve the technical quality and accuracy of your Persona's voice and likeness, including by retraining or refining the Persona-specific voice or avatar model when you provide additional Creator Content;
4. To support, debug, secure, and audit the Services;
5. To comply with applicable law (including responding to lawful requests from public authorities); and
6. For any other purpose to which you subsequently consent in writing.

We do not use biometric identifiers or biometric information derived from your Creator Content to operate any other Creator's Persona, to populate a general-purpose voice or face library available to other Creators, or to train general-purpose AI models that imitate you outside of your Persona, unless you provide separate, express written consent.

## Retention and Destruction

We retain biometric identifiers and biometric information only for as long as needed to provide the Services to you. Specifically:

- **All Creators (default rule).** Biometric identifiers and biometric information will be permanently deleted when the initial purpose for collecting or obtaining them has been satisfied (for example, when your Persona is deleted, when your account is closed, or when you withdraw your consent).
- **Illinois residents (BIPA).** Biometric identifiers and biometric information will, in any event, be permanently and irretrievably deleted no later than three (3) years following your last interaction with PersonaOn, regardless of whether the initial purpose has been satisfied earlier.
- **Texas residents (CUBI).** Biometric identifiers will, in any event, be permanently destroyed within a reasonable time and no later than the first anniversary of the date the purpose for which the biometric identifier was collected expires, unless retention beyond that period is required or permitted by other applicable law.
- **Washington residents.** Biometric identifiers will be retained no longer than is reasonably necessary for the original enrollment purpose, after which they will be permanently destroyed.
- **Quebec residents (Law 25).** Biometric identifiers and biometric information will be destroyed when the purpose for which they were collected has been achieved, in accordance with our retention schedule and as required by Article 23 of the *Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector*.
- **Canadian residents generally.** Biometric identifiers and biometric information will be destroyed in accordance with our retention schedule and PIPEDA Principle 4.5.3, no longer than necessary for the identified purposes.

We may retain backups, archives, and audit logs containing biometric identifiers or biometric information for additional limited periods consistent with our reasonable backup and audit policies, after which such data will be deleted in the ordinary course.

If a different retention period is required by applicable law (for example, in connection with active litigation, an investigation, or a regulatory hold), we will retain the data only for so long as such requirement applies and will destroy it promptly thereafter.
