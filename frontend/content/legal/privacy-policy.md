This Policy applies to information we collect through the Services. It does not address the practices of third parties we do not own or operate, even where the Services link to or interoperate with their products. "**Personal Information**" means information about an identifiable individual, including any information defined as "personal information," "personally identifiable information," or "sensitive personal information" under applicable law.

## What Personal Information We Collect

Below are the categories of Personal Information we collect, the purposes for which we collect them, and the categories of recipients with whom we may disclose them.

**Account and Profile Data** — name, email address, username, password hash, phone number, profile photo, language preference.
*Purposes:* operating, securing, and improving the Services; communicating with you; account recovery and authentication.
*Disclosed to:* service providers; Creators (where you interact with their Personas); persons you authorize.

**Identity Verification Data (Creators only)** — government-issued identification, date of birth, and similar identifiers used to verify Creator identity for trust-and-safety, anti-fraud, and tax-reporting purposes.
*Purposes:* verifying identity; complying with applicable law (including Canadian and U.S. tax-reporting obligations).
*Disclosed to:* identity-verification vendors; tax-reporting platforms.

**Payment Data** — payment card type, last four digits, billing address, billing email. Full card numbers are collected directly by our payment processor.
*Purposes:* processing transactions; fraud prevention.
*Disclosed to:* Stripe, Inc. (or replacement processor) and other financial service providers.

**Device and Network Data** — IP address, device identifiers, operating system, browser type and version, language settings, time zone, and similar technical signals.
*Purposes:* operating and securing the Services; analytics; marketing.
*Disclosed to:* hosting and analytics providers.

**Usage and Analytics Data** — pages viewed, features used, session duration, click and tap events, referral source, error logs, and performance telemetry.
*Purposes:* operating, debugging, and improving the Services; product analytics.
*Disclosed to:* analytics providers.

**Voice and Audio Data** — audio captured during interactions with Personas, voice training samples (provided by Creators), and transcripts derived from audio. Where you are a Creator, certain audio is used to train a synthetic voice model that resembles you.
*Purposes:* speech recognition and synthesis; Persona generation (Creators only); operating the Services; quality assurance.
*Disclosed to:* third-party providers of speech recognition, voice synthesis, and (where applicable) real-time communication, in each case under contractual restrictions; Creators (where you interact with their Persona).

**Image and Video Data** — photographs and videos uploaded by Creators for Persona generation; video frames captured during avatar interactions where supported.
*Purposes:* avatar generation and rendering (Creators only); operating the Services.
*Disclosed to:* third-party avatar rendering providers under contractual restrictions.

**Biometric Data (Creators only)** — voiceprints and facial geometry derived from Creator-provided audio, photo, and video Content for purposes of generating a Creator's AI Persona. Biometric data is treated as **sensitive personal information** under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, and we collect it only with your **express consent**. See our [Biometric Consent Form](/legal/biometric-consent) for details.
*Purposes:* Persona generation and operation.
*Disclosed to:* service providers contractually restricted in their use.

**Conversation Data** — the content of messages, prompts, and responses exchanged between End Users and Personas, including any Personal Information End Users voluntarily provide in conversation. Conversation Data may include sensitive topics (such as health, finances, or relationships) where End Users choose to discuss them.
*Purposes:* delivering the Services; improving Persona quality with appropriate safeguards; supporting the Creator who operates the relevant Persona.
*Disclosed to:* service providers; the Creator whose Persona is engaged in the conversation.

**Sensitive Information** — government identifiers (Creators only), biometric data (Creators only), and any health, financial, or similar sensitive information you elect to share. We collect this only with your express consent and use it only for the purposes for which you provided it.

**Communications With Us** — emails, support tickets, and other communications you send to us, and our responses.
*Purposes:* providing support; record-keeping; legal compliance.

## Why We Collect It (Purposes)

We identify the purposes for which we collect Personal Information at or before the time of collection, in accordance with PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25. The principal purposes are:

**Operating and improving the Services**, including creating and managing accounts, processing transactions, providing customer support, responding to requests, debugging, monitoring for security incidents, conducting research and analytics, and developing new features.

**Personalizing the Services**, including recommending Personas, remembering preferences, and tailoring content.

**Communicating with you**, including sending transactional messages, service notices, security alerts, and (where you have given express opt-in consent under CASL or where permitted by law) commercial electronic messages.

**Marketing and advertising**, including measuring and optimizing campaigns. We obtain consent as required under the Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation ("CASL") for commercial electronic messages.

**Legal and safety purposes**, including fulfilling legal obligations, enforcing our Terms, preventing fraud and abuse, protecting the rights and safety of users and others, responding to lawful requests from public authorities, and resolving disputes.

We will not collect new categories of Personal Information, or use Personal Information we already hold for materially different purposes, without first identifying the new purpose to you and obtaining your consent where required.

## Consent

We collect, use, and disclose Personal Information with your knowledge and consent, except where law authorizes otherwise. Depending on the sensitivity of the information and the reasonable expectations of the individual:

- **Express consent** is required for biometric data, other sensitive information, and commercial electronic messages.

## Data Security and Retention

### Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These include access controls, encryption in transit and (where applicable) at rest, monitoring, and personnel training. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong password, keep it confidential, and notify us promptly of any suspected unauthorized access.

### Retention

We retain Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law. Specific examples:

- Account profile and authentication data are retained while your account is active.
- Payment records are retained for the periods required by Canadian and applicable foreign tax and accounting laws (typically at least six years for Canadian tax purposes).
- Audio and conversation logs are retained for the periods needed to deliver the Services and improve quality, and are subject to deletion on request.
- Biometric data is governed by the retention rules in our Biometric Consent Form (no later than three years after the Creator's last interaction with the Services for Illinois residents, and otherwise for the term of the Creator's relationship with us, with destruction in accordance with applicable Canadian and U.S. law).
- De-identified or aggregated data may be retained indefinitely.

We may retain limited Personal Information for longer where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our agreements.

## Breach Notification

If we become aware of a breach of security safeguards involving your Personal Information that creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the relevant regulator (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec) as soon as feasible, in accordance with applicable law. We maintain records of breaches as required by law.
